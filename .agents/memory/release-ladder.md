The release ladder is:

- v0.6 — Notion
- v0.7 — Replit
- v0.8 — Mermaid Theme Builder
- v0.9 — BPMN for Mermaid
- v1.0 — “Zac Snyder,” the hyper-detailed synthesis of the lessons from AI-driven diagramming

v0.8 and v0.9 are concept incubators grounded in Jamie Hill's firsthand use of Mermaid through multiple LLMs. v0.8 addresses inconsistent diagram output and visual governance; v0.9 addresses the lack of a complete toolkit for collecting business-process models alongside visualizations of their written steps.

**Why:** The four v0.x releases should each isolate one specialist lesson or concept so the final v1.0 synthesis can connect them without turning every intermediate post into an unwieldy rewrite. The concept-incubator framing prevents either adjacent project from being overstated as a finished product.

**How to apply:** Treat each numbered release as a focused chapter with one primary source, one proof artifact, and one lesson; preserve the council taxonomy and round results as shared context rather than re-running the competition.
