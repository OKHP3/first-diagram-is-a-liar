# Editorial Acceptance Checklist

Use this checklist before publishing any article v1.x update, new council
round, deck, or diagram derivative.

- [ ] Update [`release-manifest.md`](release-manifest.md) first: stable ID,
  stage, date (or “date not recorded”), purpose, owner, source, render, and
  status are present.
- [ ] Confirm whether the change is an article release, a diagram iteration,
  a competition round, or a separate production artifact. Do not use V1/V2 as
  article versions.
- [ ] Use only the canonical result language: Copilot V1 is Round 1 top
  performer; Claude V2 is Round 2 top performer; no overall winner.
- [ ] Preserve the tiered roster and disclose unequal access or late-entry
  conditions. Notion remains a specialty synthesizer; Replit remains a
  late-entry specialty role.
- [ ] Attribute human authorship and editorial decisions to Jamie Hill /
  OverKill Hill P³™; label model-generated contributions by provider.
- [ ] Add each diagram to `diagram-manifest.csv` with source, rendered PNG,
  public link, and status. Verify the source renders before publication.
- [ ] Check every external logo, image, referral, and support link for
  attribution, permission status, and labeling.
- [ ] Keep the checked-in source and PNG available when a third-party render is
  unavailable.
- [ ] Reconcile the article, decks, gallery, archive, and captions with this
  manifest before release. Resolve discrepancies explicitly; do not silently
  guess missing dates or historical facts.
## Story and claims

- [ ] The release identifier and purpose match `release-manifest.md`.
- [ ] Council counts use the tier taxonomy, not an unexplained total.
- [ ] Round winners are labelled by round and are supported by archived source.
- [ ] Exhibition and Specialty entries are not presented as direct competitors.
- [ ] Historical references such as AutoCAD are not counted as participants.

## Provenance

- [ ] Human author, orchestrator, editor, and adjudicator are identified.
- [ ] Model/provider contributions are labelled without implying human authorship.
- [ ] Every public diagram has a local source and manifest row.
- [ ] Third-party logos, images, referrals, and support links are reviewed.

## Delivery

- [ ] Article, archive README, deck captions, member notes, and campaign copy
  use the same release and winner language.
- [ ] Hosted Mermaid links are checked and local fallback sources are present.
- [ ] Article metadata, canonical URL, social image, and alt text are verified.
- [ ] Link, render, accessibility, and analytics checks have recorded results.

## Decision

- [ ] Accepted as v1.x update.
- [ ] Held as an internal/archive change.
- [ ] Rejected or returned for evidence, attribution, or delivery fixes.
