## Synthesis spine

The final cut connects the full learning sequence:

1. build context before asking for output;
2. use a precise prompt protocol and disclose conditions;
3. revise through disagreement instead of hiding the first pass;
4. make structural truth visible through loops, forks, and honest failures;
5. preserve sources, renders, and a correction path so the work stays
   maintainable;
6. govern the visual language after structure becomes cheap;
7. choose notation that can carry the meaning of the work;
8. keep human adjudication responsible for the final claim; and
9. ship with local fallbacks rather than trusting a third-party render alone.

The detailed evidence-to-claim route is
[`v1.0-evidence-index.md`](v1.0-evidence-index.md). The final article must use
the canonical result language: Copilot V1 led Round 1, Claude V2 led Round 2,
and no overall council winner is declared.
