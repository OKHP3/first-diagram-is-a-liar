## Core argument

The source begins with AutoCAD R10 and the manual discipline of constructing a
visual line by line. It connects that history to the cost of forcing ideas into
structure by hand: attention, effort, delay, and the loss of the original
thought while wrestling the canvas.

ROY is introduced as the amount of understanding produced per unit of
explanation invested. A picture is not automatically worth a thousand words.
The question is whether the visual returns enough clarity, compression, and
shared understanding to justify the words that produced it.

## Council brief

The same brief went to multiple systems independently. The requested outputs
were a three-part thesis, a first Mermaid diagram, and three failure modes.
After the independent runs, the process was to compare, adjudicate, and
synthesize.

The source's compact sequence is:

`words -> structure -> systems -> leverage`
