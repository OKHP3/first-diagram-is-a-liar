## Notion's distinct role

Notion is described as the documentarian, memoir keeper, and consolidation
surface for a multi-model effort. It is not treated as another direct model
competitor. Its value is architectural: preserving the prompt, versions,
decisions, drafts, and receipts so a later session can resume without
reconstructing the entire experiment from memory.

## Reusable product implications

- A PRD is more durable than a one-line prompt because it names goals,
  constraints, evaluation criteria, output formats, and relationships between
  artifacts.
- A writing companion should preserve a working draft while allowing other
  tools to contribute bounded criticism, structure, or compression.
- A source room should make lineage navigable without pretending that storage
  alone creates understanding.
- Notion belongs in a Specialty condition when the council is evaluated, with
  its different role kept visible rather than flattened into a leaderboard.
