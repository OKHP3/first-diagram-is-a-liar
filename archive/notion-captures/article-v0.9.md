## Source position

This page is a short outline about the gap exposed after the diagram: language
models can make structure cheaply, but consistent visual language and style
governance remain difficult.

## Outline

- Structure is cheap; style governance is not.
- Mermaid AI partially solves generation but does not provide the complete
  visual-language contract.
- Mermaid Theme Builder is the proposed stopgap: generate diagrams anywhere,
  then standardize their look and feel.
- A future soft launch and next-step plan are expected, but are not confirmed
  here as deployed work.
