## Prompt sequence

The eight prompts move from idea discovery to evaluation:

1. Seed the ROY question.
2. Turn the question into a meta-level diagramming problem.
3. Expose the happy-path lie and request revision loops.
4. Lock voice, brand, and restrained visual treatment.
5. Formalize the council and comparative evaluation.
6. Request a best-of hybrid rather than another isolated draft.
7. Explain the origin story in a public comment.
8. Run the diagram competition and make the judgment inspectable.

The useful fairness statement is: the brief was consistent, but the conditions
were not. The source calls out different access tiers, Notion's documentarian
role, and Replit's late arrival rather than flattening them into one score.

## Launch logic

The public hub moves from the AutoCAD and Visio manual labor tax, through ROY,
attention limits, Mermaid as text-native structure, and the difference between
Mermaid syntax and a Mermaid AI platform. It then moves into public comparison:
the deck makes the field visible, polls add an audience signal, and a later
debrief compares audience, human, and council judgment.
