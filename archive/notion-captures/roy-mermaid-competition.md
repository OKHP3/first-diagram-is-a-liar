## Purpose

This page is the consolidation point for the ROY and Mermaid experiment. It
keeps the evolving thesis, the winning artifacts, the early LinkedIn post and
article deliverables, and the messy lineage that made the later synthesis
possible.

The narrator's role is deliberately modest: keep the record, preserve the
versions, and make the experiment inspectable after the chat windows close.

## Source-derived method

- **Thesis:** a proverb is not a metric. A visual earns its place when the
  clarity it returns justifies the cognitive effort spent making it.
- **ROY:** Return on Your Words. Useful signals include fewer follow-up
  questions, fewer rework loops, and fewer translation meetings.
- **Mechanism:** words become structure, structure becomes a system, and the
  system can create leverage.
- **Honesty constraint:** a clean linear path is often a tidy lie. Forks,
  loopbacks, uncertainty, and revision belong in the model when they belong in
  the work.
- **Council method:** run the same brief independently, compare the outputs,
  adjudicate signal versus noise, and synthesize a stronger hybrid.

## Hybrid synthesis notes

The source assigns different useful instincts to the council: lived history and
ROY framing, architecture and the manual labor tax, systems framing and
decision gates, restraint and pacing, and controlled reduction. The result is
not a winner copied from one model. It is a human-selected combination of
perspectives.

## Early deliverables

The source records an article v0.1, a short LinkedIn post, an initial comment,
and a Mermaid diagram with three lanes: Prose, Visual, and Ship. The diagram's
ROY gate branches toward shipping or revision, then feeds reader comments back
into a later version.
