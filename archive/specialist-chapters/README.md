## Packet contract

Each packet contains exactly one:

- one-sentence claim;
- primary source;
- proof artifact;
- lesson;
- proposed article update;
- LinkedIn companion post and tracked canonical destination.

These are chapter packets, not four new article releases. The current external
article remains `ARTICLE-0.5`; the local `ARTICLE-1.0` editorial cut remains
prepared and unpublished. The v0.6 through v0.9 labels identify the planned
LinkedIn companion series and must not be rewritten as `ARTICLE-0.6` through
`ARTICLE-0.9`.

## Evidence boundary

Notion is the editorial workspace for the v0.6 source. Its public-safe working
copy is exported into repository Markdown; private Notion URLs and page IDs are
not required for collaborators to inspect this packet. GitHub is the public-safe
archive and immutable release record. The Mermaid Theme Builder and BPMN for
Mermaid are adjacent concept incubators, not additions to the original council
roster or its round results.

External destinations are recorded as destinations and proof surfaces, not as
owned deployments. A live HTTP result does not by itself prove a LinkedIn
publication or transfer authorship to a model or provider.
