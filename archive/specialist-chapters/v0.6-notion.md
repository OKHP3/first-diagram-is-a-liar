## Lesson

Notion did create diagrams, but Jamie's account is that its main value in this
effort was keeping the room's memory: the requirements, model outputs,
interviews, decisions, and source receipts. In Jamie's setup at the time,
Notion AI used lower-cost, earlier-generation models, so its diagrams were not
treated as a like-for-like comparison with the frontier-model submissions.
Jamie also used Notion's MCP connections to carry the brief across platforms
without repeating it from scratch. Notion remained a distinct Specialty role,
not a Core Five judge.
