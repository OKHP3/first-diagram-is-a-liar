## One-sentence claim

AI made Mermaid structure cheap, but consistent visual language still needs a
renderer-aware governance layer that treats styling as a reusable contract.

## Lesson

When structure is generated cheaply, the next bottleneck is visual governance:
family-aware hierarchy, reusable palettes, and renderer-aware output that
survives beyond one convenient preview.
