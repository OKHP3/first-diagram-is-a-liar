PROJECT IDENTITY

This repository teaches:

- why the first diagram is usually a liar
- ROY: Return on Your Words
- the difference between a tidy happy-path diagram and an honest diagram
- visible doubt, revision, feedback, and return paths
- structured disagreement across differently conditioned AI outputs
- making a reusable artifact instead of writing an exercise in hypocrisy

The repository has two surfaces:

1. Active application:
   A client-only TypeScript, React, Vite, and Tailwind single-page tutorial.

2. Evidence archive:
   The preserved article source, eight prompts, Mermaid V1/V2 diagrams, rendered assets, Council records, slides, provenance, and release evidence under archive/.
