# Three-loop review contract

Status: candidate preparation only. Date: 2026-09-29.

Question: Do the recommendations and resulting publication candidates provide a coherent, source-supported 7,000-12,000-word practitioner article with business strategy and tactical implementation, while preserving diagram V1/V2 and removing serial-release scaffolding?

## Acceptance criteria

1. One argument, 7,000-12,000 words, under LinkedIn's verified 125,000-character limit; no padding to meet a quota.
2. V1/V2 remain diagram rounds. Article release labels, repeated introductions, stale future promises, and repeated conclusions are removed from essay prose.
3. Claims distinguish observations, author judgments, hypotheses, and proposed business methods. No fabricated scoring matrix, productivity improvement, audience funnel, or production capability.
4. Core Five / Specialty / Exhibition / Attempted conditions and contributor credit are preserved. Source/render stage differences remain visible.
5. Corporate readers can act: entry criteria, roles, work products, exception handling, gates, stopping rule, measurement, and maintenance owner.
6. Public-safe citations and artifacts only. No private Notion URLs/IDs, employer details, credentials, or raw private captures in the public packet.
7. Website, LinkedIn, and Notion candidates share the substantive master; documented presentation differences only. Nothing implies they have been published.
8. No em dashes in new prose; retain natural, practical, builder-oriented voice, short purposeful lines, and one decisive close.

## Loop sequence

- Loop 1: challenge the preceding editorial recommendations and initial claim ledger before drafting. Artifact: `../editorial-review-2026-09-29.md`, SHA256 `CA32AE60079161BC2754B9E980898906B9135B7A3849C5CB6B76E381A9A3B61C`.
- Loop 2: independently review the frozen unified manuscript, source ledger, and announcement; revise surviving defects.
- Loop 3: independently review the final frozen surface candidates, cross-surface parity, formatting constraints, and disposition of earlier findings.

Each loop runs evidence, outcome, and safety-portability roles in separate contexts. Material disagreement goes to root adjudication. Material agreement triggers a narrow falsification review, followed by adjudication. Role reports are analytical; source and character checks can be live measurements. No protected empirical holdout is available. Review cannot establish causal business uplift or general model superiority. Reviewers must not edit the artifact they judge.

The lead writer adjudicates and revises. This creates an acknowledged author/adjudicator correlation; independent review is an aid, not human owner acceptance. Final disposition approves candidate delivery only. Live article publication remains separate.
