# LinkedIn Proto-Post v0.6: Notion, the Writer's Room Behind the Council

**Status:** Proto-post for review

**Release:** v0.6
**Primary angle:** Notion as the memory and consolidation layer behind the AI council

## Draft post

Notion was not another model in my Mermaid experiment.

It was the room the experiment happened in.

ChatGPT was my workbench. Claude was my structural critic. The rest of the council generated and challenged diagrams. Notion did the less glamorous job that became one of the most important: it kept the work from evaporating when the chat windows closed.

I used it to hold the PRD, version the article, consolidate model outputs, record interviews, and connect the narrative to Mermaid sources in GitHub.

That made Notion more than a note-taking app. It became the memory layer between AI platforms.

The hard part of a multi-model experiment is not getting seven answers. It is finding the answer you need three weeks later, knowing where it came from, and being able to revise it without losing the thread.

The pattern I am taking forward is simple:

AI conversations generate possibilities.

Notion turns them into a navigable working record.

GitHub preserves the artifacts and receipts.

Neither surface is enough by itself.

That is why Notion gets its own release in *The First Diagram Is Usually a Liar*. Not because it won the diagram competition, but because it made the competition reusable.

Where does your work live after you close the chat window?
