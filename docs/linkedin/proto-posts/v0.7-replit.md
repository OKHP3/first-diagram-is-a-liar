## Draft post

Replit is not another chatbot in this experiment.

It entered as a late-entry builder perspective, with accumulated context. That
condition matters: Replit is archived as a specialty contribution, outside the
Core Five comparison. It is not a cold-start result or a tie.

That changed the useful question.

A chat answer can be judged as an answer. A build environment has to be judged
against the artifact and the work around it. The Replit archive pushed me to
ask whether the diagram's insight could move from explanation toward something
inspectable and runnable.

The practical lesson I am carrying forward is simple: inspect what is there,
agree what may change, make the smallest useful change, then verify the result
against the real artifact.

That is a working discipline, not a feature claim about any one platform. And
this archive does not establish a product release or public deployment.

Tool category is part of the prompt. Give the environment the right job, clear
boundaries, and a test that matches the work.

What changes when you treat an AI environment as a place to work on an artifact,
not just talk about one?
