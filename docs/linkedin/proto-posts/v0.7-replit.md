## Draft post

When I started the ROY and Mermaid experiment, I thought Replit was just
another AI. I was only beginning to use it. I did not yet see it as the build
platform I do now.

That changed over time.

In the original diagram exercise, Replit arrived after the others and had
context from their earlier attempts. Its diagrams looked comparable to me,
but this was not a like-for-like result. The timing and context differed, and
Replit was not playing the same general-knowledge assistant role as the others.

That is useful context, not a Replit win, tie, or cold-start comparison. It was
a late-entry specialty contribution, outside the Core Five.

The bigger surprise came later. The product changed, and I kept finding Replit
more capable than I had expected. After the diagram exercise, I used it to
build single-page web applications. Those projects came after the proof. They
showed me what I had missed: Replit is a build surface. The work is not only
asking for a good answer. It is shaping an artifact, then checking what
actually works.

I would not reach for Replit just to edit Mermaid files. But if I were
building a single-page app that creates Mermaid diagrams, Replit would be my
pick. That is my current judgment, not a result of the earlier comparison.

There is more to come on custom applications for Mermaid. For now, sometimes
you do not find a tool's real job until the next project.

The original experiment, with its conditions and receipts, is here:
https://overkillhill.com/writings/first-diagram-is-a-liar/
