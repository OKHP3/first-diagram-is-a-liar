## Draft post

The fair test is not always the same prompt and the same scorecard.

Sometimes the fair test is admitting that you treated a different instrument
like one you already knew.

That was my Replit lesson.

Replit arrived later, and I was less familiar with the build environment. I
had not given it a planning brief that made the job and boundaries clear. I was
still judging a build environment by answer quality.

Category error.

A chatbot can help you think through an artifact.

An agentic build environment is a different instrument. Its fair test has to
look at the artifact and the conditions around the work.

Replit was a late-entry specialty contribution, outside the Core Five
comparison. It came with accumulated context. That is not a cold-start result
or a tie.

For this kind of work, I now inspect what exists, agree on boundaries, make
the smallest useful change, and test the result against the real artifact.

That is a lesson about how I approached the work. The archived submission is
not a product release or proof of public deployment.

Not every AI tool is another voice in the room.
Some are part of the room's construction crew.

The broader experiment is here:
https://overkillhill.com/writings/first-diagram-is-a-liar/
