# Editorial Acceptance Checklist

Use this checklist before calling a future public update v1.x.

## Story and claims

- [ ] The release identifier and purpose match `canonical-story.md`.
- [ ] Council counts use the tier taxonomy, not an unexplained total.
- [ ] Round winners are labelled by round and are supported by archived source.
- [ ] Exhibition and Specialty entries are not presented as direct competitors.
- [ ] Historical references such as AutoCAD are not counted as participants.

## Provenance

- [ ] Human author, orchestrator, editor, and adjudicator are identified.
- [ ] Model/provider contributions are labelled without implying human authorship.
- [ ] Every public diagram has a local source and manifest row.
- [ ] Third-party logos, images, referrals, and support links are reviewed.

## Delivery

- [ ] Article, archive README, deck captions, member notes, and campaign copy
  use the same release and winner language.
- [ ] Hosted Mermaid links are checked and local fallback sources are present.
- [ ] Article metadata, canonical URL, social image, and alt text are verified.
- [ ] Link, render, accessibility, and analytics checks have recorded results.

## Decision

- [ ] Accepted as v1.x update.
- [ ] Held as an internal/archive change.
- [ ] Rejected or returned for evidence, attribution, or delivery fixes.
